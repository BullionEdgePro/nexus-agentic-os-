WhatsApp links to publish

Every link below was generated from the live registry and passed through the platform's own router. Each one is confirmed to reach the business that publishes it — not the triage menu. Four of these are the pending website edits. Zipicka is listed too because it owns the number and the same tag applies — its own link is worth publishing for the same reason.

Why the #tag matters. All five businesses answer on one WhatsApp number. Without the tag the switchboard cannot tell which firm a stranger wants, so it has to ask — and the customer picks from a menu of five before anyone can help them.

The tag is prefilled in the message box and the customer sends it without thinking about it. It only counts at the start of a message: someone writing "do you know anything about #juris-prime" mid-sentence is not treated as arriving from a link.

Where to put them

  1. The main Contact or Enquire button in the header, so it is on every page.
  2. The footer, beside the phone number you already publish.
  3. Any "Speak to a lawyer" / "Book a viewing" call to action.
  4. Your Google Business Profile, as the website or appointment link.

Each business needs only its own link. Test one from a phone that has never messaged the number before — the reply should come from that business directly, with no menu in between.
